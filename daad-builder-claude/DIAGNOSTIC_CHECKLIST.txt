==============================================================================
UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM:
==============================================================================

The template buttons (+ Basic Verbs, + Movement, + Interaction) only add
VERB words to vocabulary. They don't add:
- The NOUN words (holloway, city, etc.)
- The RULES that make commands work
- The LOCATIONS where the game happens
- The OBJECTS you can interact with
- The MESSAGES that are displayed

You MUST load the complete game file to get all of these!
